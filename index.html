About us

A small team in Dehradun, building for what's next.

SkyShield Technology is a startup based in Dehradun, India — the same mountain terrain in the screenshots throughout this site, not a studio render or a stock demo. What you've seen on this site is what actually runs, flown and tested on ground we know.

We build the aircraft, the ground station and the analytics ourselves, as one system, because that's what we think autonomous aerial intelligence actually needs to become useful — not three products stitched together after the fact, but one team answering for the whole thing, built for where the field is heading rather than just what's easiest to ship today.
