Remote Video Terminal — Multi-Drone Viewer

Every drone in the air, on one screen.

A control room, a command post or a base can watch several SkyShield ground stations at once: live video, the map, the AI boxes and the alerts of each drone, side by side in a browser. It is free, it is one small file, and it needs no internet connection.

What it does

One viewer for the whole operation

  • All streams together — the screen opens on a grid of every drone you have added. Double-click a tile, or press its Full button, to give one drone the whole screen; Esc brings the grid back.
  • Not only video — each drone brings its map position and track, the AI detection boxes and its alerts. An alert on a drone you are not looking at is still shown.
  • Daylight and thermal — switch each drone between its EO and IR camera from the viewer, without disturbing the operator.
  • Flight reports — when a flight ends, its report (flight ID, place, route and figures) can be opened from the same screen.
  • Made for a wall screen — on a large or 4K display the buttons, names and alerts grow with the screen, and the controls always name the drone they act on.
  • Watch only — the viewer cannot command an aircraft or change anything on a ground station.

How to use it

Running in about two minutes.

  1. Download and unzip the viewer on the computer that will show the drones.
  2. Open index.html by double-clicking it. It opens in your browser. Chrome or Edge is recommended.
  3. On each ground station, open Video Terminal from the main menu, turn on Enable Remote Video Terminal and press Copy link.
  4. In the viewer, press Add drone, paste the link and give the drone a name. Repeat for each ground station.
  5. Watch. The drones are remembered, so next time the viewer opens they connect by themselves.

To show it on other screens too (a tablet, a second PC), run Start RVT Multi.cmd from the same folder. It serves the viewer to your local network and prints the address to open on the other devices. This needs Python installed on that one computer.

What you need

Good to know before you start.

The same networkThe viewer and the ground stations must be able to reach each other: the same LAN or Wi-Fi, or the same VPN for a base in another city. No internet is needed.
SkyShield GCS on each droneEach drone is flown from a SkyShield ground station with the Remote Video Terminal turned on. The viewer joins each one as an ordinary viewer.
A link per ground stationThe link carries an access key. By default a ground station makes a new key each time its video terminal is turned on, and the drone must then be added again with the new link.
One viewer place per droneThe viewer uses one of the ground station's viewer places (four by default) and receives each drone's video the whole time, so switching between drones is instant.

Why is it a download and not a page on this website? This website is served over a secure connection (https), and browsers do not allow a secure page to connect to a ground station on your own network. Opened from your computer, the viewer has no such limit. Your video never passes through this website or any SkyShield server: it goes straight from each ground station to your screen.
