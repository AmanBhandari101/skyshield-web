Privacy

What this website records

If you accept the cookie notice, this site counts visits and records how pages are used, so we can see what is useful and fix what is not. If you decline, nothing is recorded.

What is collected

  • Pages viewed, time spent, how far down a page you scrolled, and where you clicked.
  • Which file you downloaded and whether you clicked an email or LinkedIn link.
  • Approximate location (country and city, from the network), browser, device type and the site that linked here.

This is done with Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. Clarity can replay a visit as a recording of the page; text typed into forms is masked and never recorded. Neither is used for advertising, and we do not sell or share this information.

What is not collected

Your name and email address are only known to us if you email us. They are used only to reply to you.

Changing your choice

Your choice is kept in this browser.
