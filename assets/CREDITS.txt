SkyShield Technology — image credits
=====================================

Every image on this site is SkyShield's own: product screenshots, screen recordings,
the airframe render and the pipeline diagram. No third-party photography is used.

Of particular note, because they are the strongest claims the site makes — both are
real captures from the ground station, shown on the GCS page's window walk-through
(Situational awareness, after the Video window) rather than the Video Analytics page:

  gcs-sar-flood.jpg      Flood-response scenario. The 23-object count, the per-person
                         track numbers and confidences, the group ring and the SAR
                         evacuation alert are produced by the software itself — they
                         are not drawn by the web page.

  gcs-mine-rollover.jpg  Quarry incident. The truck detection, its confidence and the
                         "Vehicle Rollover, Critical Event" alert are the same:
                         genuine detector and rule-engine output, not illustration.

An earlier revision of this page used three Pexels photographs with detection boxes
and alert banners rendered in CSS over them. Those were correctly licensed (Pexels
permits commercial use without attribution), but the overlays were illustrative
rather than real detector output, which is a poor thing to put in front of a
procurement audience. They were removed in favour of the two genuine captures above.

The airframe render (ravin-render.png) is a concept render of the Ravin, not a
photograph of the built aircraft. It matches the production CAD geometry.
